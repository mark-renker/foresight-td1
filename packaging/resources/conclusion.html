Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Black Tie Ventures > Foresight TD-1.

The plug-in reports its lookahead as latency, so your DAW compensates for it automatically. Set Lookahead to 0 for a zero-latency mode.
