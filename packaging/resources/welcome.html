Foresight TD-1 @VERSION@

An anticipatory transient designer: the detector runs ahead of the audio (lookahead), with ratio-normalised level-independent detection, transient-only spectral tilt, Mid/Side targeting, a Pre control for the milliseconds before each onset, a bleed-focus Threshold and the Foresight Lens hit-locked envelope display.

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3, standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; it appears under Black Tie Ventures > Foresight TD-1.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
